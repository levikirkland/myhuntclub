# 🦌 My Hunt Club
### *Precision Management for the Modern Outdoorsman*

**My Hunt Club** is a comprehensive, high-performance platform built to streamline the logistics of hunting leases, clubs, and land management. It replaces outdated spreadsheets and paper maps with a sleek, digital-first interface designed for both the field and the front office.

---

## 💎 The Problem Solved
Managing a hunting club involves complex safety logistics, financial tracking, and member communication. **My Hunt Club** centralizes these variables into a single "Source of Truth," ensuring safer hunts, organized finances, and better land stewardship.

## 🚀 Core Functionality
* **Live Check-In System:** Real-time visibility of member locations for maximum safety and hunt coordination.
* **Property Mapping:** Integrated stand locations, food plots, and boundary tracking.
* **Member Portal:** Automated dues, digital waivers, and contact management.
* **Harvest Analytics:** Log and track game data to monitor herd health and club success.
* **Financial Dashboard:** Full transparency for club expenses and lease payments.
